# Brand Details for This Project

Use the following copy-ready brand brief for training an agent or generating content for this project.

## 1. Project Description

I'm a Cloud/DevOps Engineer and AI Automation Specialist, with 7+ years of experience building, deploying, and scaling production-grade systems. My background in PHP/Laravel evolved into full ownership of cloud infrastructure, automation workflows, and system reliability.

I design and operate scalable systems using AWS, DigitalOcean, Docker, CI/CD (GitHub Actions), Linux, and Nginx. I build automation workflows (n8n, APIs, AI-assisted processes) that eliminate repetitive tasks, improve operational efficiency, and reduce infrastructure costs for businesses..

## 2. Brand Positioning

The brand is positioned as a thoughtful, technically strong, and future-focused developer brand. It should feel polished, credible, and modern without being overly flashy. The overall impression is: smart, dependable, practical, and ambitious.

## 3. Visual Identity: Colors

Primary color:

- Blue: #2563eb
- Secondary blue accents: #60a5fa, #1d4ed8

Neutral tones:

- Dark text: #18181b
- Light background: #fafafa
- Soft gray: #f4f4f5
- Dark mode background: #09090b

Visual style guidance:

- Use blue as the main accent color for emphasis, links, buttons, and highlights.
- Use neutral grays and whites for clean layouts and readable content.
- Keep the design minimal, modern, and high-contrast.

## 4. Typography

Primary font:

- Instrument Sans

Secondary font:

- JetBrains Mono

Typography guidance:

- Use Instrument Sans for body text, headings, and general UI.
- Use JetBrains Mono for technical references, code snippets, and developer-focused content.
- The overall type style should feel clean, modern, and slightly premium.

## 5. Tone and Personality

Tone:

- Confident
- Calm
- Technical
- Approachable
- Professional
- Pragmatic
- Optimistic
- Thoughtful

Personality traits:

- Reliable and disciplined
- Curious and growth-minded
- Clear and insightful
- Builder-oriented
- Slightly understated, not overly promotional

Writing style guidance:

- Prefer clear, direct language over hype.
- Sound intelligent, experienced, and grounded.
- Emphasize quality, reliability, maintainability, and continuous improvement.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, gimmicks, or overly casual language.

## 7. Short Brand Summary

A modern, engineering-first personal brand for a software engineer and DevOps professional who builds scalable systems, embraces cloud and AI automation, and communicates with clarity, confidence, and technical depth.
